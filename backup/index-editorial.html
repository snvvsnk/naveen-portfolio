About

I've spent most of my career on the parts of e-commerce platforms customers never see directly — catalog systems, search infrastructure, pricing pipelines, and the backend plumbing that keeps millions of products indexed, priced, and discoverable. I started on Oracle ATG and Endeca, led the migration of monolithic commerce systems to AWS-native microservices, and now work on catalog and AI/ML integration for a fashion & home retail platform.

My applied-AI work goes back further than people often assume: I started by fine-tuning classification models on AWS SageMaker, moved into LLM fine-tuning and OpenAI integration for production ranking pipelines, and most recently customized CLAUDE.md configuration and authored multiple SKILLS.md files to create reusable Claude/Cursor skills that streamlined development workflows — enabling the product team to focus on feature development. It's now being used as the base for a broader, org-wide rollout of this approach.

I care about systems that are boring in production — observable, well-instrumented, and easy to hand off — and I like mentoring engineers into being the kind of on-call partner you actually want at 2am.
