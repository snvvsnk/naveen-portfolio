about

Backend by trade, AI by recent necessity

I started my career in 2010 at Tata Consultancy Services in Chennai, straight out of college, building telecom e-commerce features for NII Holdings' Latin American markets — profile management, self-care applications, billing integration. Within a couple of years I was leading a 5-person module on a telecom platform build. From there I moved to Sapient in Bengaluru, joining the team migrating JCPenney's $1.6B+ e-commerce business off legacy infrastructure onto Oracle ATG — catalog, pricing, and product data integration at a scale I hadn't touched before.

A stint at TOMS Shoes in LA followed, then back to JCPenney — this time on the SRE side at Publicis Sapient, where I spent five years owning production health: building internal tooling (an ELK-based monitoring dashboard, Python automation for catalog/pricing feeds, on-the-fly cache and price-correction tools), migrating monolithic ATG systems to AWS microservices, and eventually leading the Top-of-Funnel SRE team from onsite. That's also where I've spent most of my career on the parts of e-commerce platforms customers never see directly — catalog systems, search infrastructure, pricing pipelines, and the backend plumbing that keeps millions of products indexed, priced, and discoverable.

In 2021 I moved to Rakuten Rewards to lead catalog and AI/ML integration for a fashion & home retail platform, and I'm now at Rakuten Marketing. My applied-AI work goes back further than people often assume: I started by fine-tuning classification models on AWS SageMaker, moved into LLM fine-tuning and OpenAI integration for production ranking pipelines, and most recently customized CLAUDE.md configuration and authored multiple SKILLS.md files to create reusable Claude/Cursor skills that streamlined development workflows — enabling the product team to focus on feature development. It's now being used as the base for a broader, org-wide rollout of this approach.

I care about systems that are boring in production — observable, well-instrumented, and easy to hand off — and I like mentoring engineers into being the kind of on-call partner you actually want at 2am.
